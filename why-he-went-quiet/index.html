why did he go quiet?

Silence means different things from different charts. Some men go quiet while deciding, some after deciding, and some are not quiet at all — just slow.

Silence is not one behaviour

A decisive chart goes quiet because the decision is already made. An adaptive one goes quiet drafting three replies and sending none. Same silence on your phone. Opposite meanings.

What to actually watch

Not the gap itself — the shape of it. Does he explain it afterwards? Does the plan get vaguer, or just quieter? One data point is a mood. The same behaviour three times is a pattern.

What a chart will not tell you

Whether he comes back. Nothing here predicts an event, and anyone who says otherwise is selling something worse than this.

And if the silence has already ended, the other half of this reading is why he came back when he did.
