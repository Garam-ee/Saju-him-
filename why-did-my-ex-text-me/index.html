why did my ex text me after months?

Four things move a man back into your messages, and only one of them is about you. Three are timing — his year, his month, and the element his chart has been short of the whole time.

a text arrives. weather moved first

In saju, almost nothing lands on a random Tuesday. Time is counted in pillars — year, month, day, hour — and every pillar is two letters pressing on the eight you were born with. When two men send the same hey in the same week, they are usually answering two different letters.

So the question is not why did he text me. It is what moved, and in which of his letters. Three of the four answers are in his timing. Only the last one is about his feelings.

the year and the month do different jobs

세운, seun — the year pillar — is the big weather. It runs from ipchun (입춘, early February), not January 1, and it sets the subject of his whole year: work, money, family, or somebody.

월운, wolun — the month pillar — turns at each solar term, roughly the 4th to the 8th of the month. This is the detail people miss. A text on the 3rd and a text on the 6th can belong to two different months, and two different letters.

If he went quiet in spring and messaged in November, the first thing worth knowing is which of his letters November is holding.

the missing element comes back looking for a season

how to tell an ego check from unfinished business

Four tests, in this order. You can run three of them before you reply.

1. Where does the message point? Ego points at him — his week, his memory, his joke. Alignment points at you or at a plan.

2. Does a time appear within two exchanges? Real intent produces a specific: Thursday, 7, that place. Ego produces we should catch up sometime and survives for eleven days on it.

3. Which star is the month carrying for him? If the month hands him 정재, the clean wealth star, the pull is aimed and it behaves like a girlfriend, not a fling. If it hands him 겁재, the robbing one, he is not being called to you. He is being called to a contest.

4. What was happening in your life the week he texted? If the honest answer is nothing, I just posted, that is your data. Ego arrives when your life gets loud. Alignment arrives when his does.

the hour he sent it is not a detail

Read the timestamp against his day master.

A water day master sends at 2am, in paragraphs, and remembers a song. A metal day master sends three words at noon and finds the whole thing embarrassing by evening. The same text means five different things depending on who typed it. This is the part a screenshot cannot tell you and a chart can.

what a chart will not tell you

Whether he has changed. Whether he means it this time. Whether you should answer at all.

Saju gives you the reason and the pattern. It does not take the decision off your hands, and a reading that offers to is selling you a worse product than this one. The three-times rule still holds: one message is weather, the same message every season for a year is a structure. If he has gone quiet on you before, that silence has its own reading, and the two should be read together.

quick answers

Does saju say my ex will come back? No. It names the months when his relationship palace is being touched, which is when the pattern is most likely to move. A pull is not a promise.
