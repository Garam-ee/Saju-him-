what am I short on?

Every saju chart weighs out across five elements. The one you have least of is the one you keep looking for in other people.

The five elements

A chart is read as wood, fire, earth, metal and water. You are not one of them — you are a mix, and the mix is rarely even.

The one you lack

In Korean the shortage has a name: yongsin (용신). It is the thing your chart reaches for. People who carry a lot of it feel unreasonably good to be around, and that is not a coincidence.

Why the birth hour matters

The hour is a whole quarter of the chart. Two people born the same day two hours apart weigh out differently — which is why a calculator that only asks for the date is telling you about the day, not you.
