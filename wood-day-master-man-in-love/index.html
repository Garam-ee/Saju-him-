wood day master man in love

Wood doesn't move toward what it wants. It grows toward it, which looks like patience from the outside and feels like a wall from the inside. Two woods get called one type. They aren't.

first, which wood is he

His 일간, ilgan — the day master — is one letter: the heavenly stem of the day he was born. Everything else in a saju chart is read against it, the way a sentence is read against its subject. Ten stems exist. Two of them are wood, and they are not variations on a theme. They are two different strategies for surviving.

갑목, gap mok (甲) is yang wood: the oak, the pine, the timber tree. It grows straight up, decides early, and would rather break than bow. 을목, eul mok (乙) is yin wood: the vine, the flower, the grass that comes up through a crack in a car park. It bends, wraps, finds the way around, and outlives almost everything.

Most "wood man" descriptions online are written for the element and not for the pole, so they land on the tree and leave the vine with a personality it doesn't have. Read the wrong one and you will diagnose a man you have never met.

how a tree says he's in

He doesn't say it. He puts you in the plan. A 갑목 man in love starts rearranging logistics about eight weeks before he uses a word like us: the trip is booked for a date that suits your job, the dinner is moved because you had a bad week, the two friends whose opinion he actually respects have met you. Nothing is announced. Everything is built.

His affection is load-bearing. You don't notice it until something heavy lands on it and it doesn't move. That is the appeal and also the trap: a tree that holds you up will not notice it is standing on you.

What he will probably never do is the paragraph. If you need the sentence, ask for it directly. He will find the request embarrassing, and he will answer it, badly and honestly, and that answer is worth more than one from a man who writes beautifully every Tuesday.

how a vine says he's in

He notices. The coffee order, the meeting you were dreading, the song you mentioned once in a car. Then he adapts before you ask: he eats at your restaurant, shifts dinner around your Tuesday, learns which of your friends not to joke about. It feels like being known, and to an 을목 man it is the same thing as saying it.

The problem is that this is also how he treats people he isn't in love with. English has no word for the gap between warm and interested, and a vine lives in that gap for months. The tell isn't enthusiasm. It's repetition. He does the small thing again next week, then next month, and the same trellis keeps getting climbed.

The cost runs the other way: he gives until there is nothing left to give, and then detaches without a scene, so you find out late. If you are with a vine, ask what he wants occasionally. He will be startled. He will also tell you.

the quiet that isn't losing interest

Wood grows toward what it wants but never at it. So when a wood man goes still, the useful question is not how does he feel. It is what is directly above his head.

Three ceilings do it. Being managed — his calendar, his money, his friendships handled for him. Being rushed to name a future he hadn't finished thinking about. Being corrected in front of other people, which is the one a tree cannot forgive, because a tree cannot be pruned in public.

He does not fight a ceiling. He stops growing under it, or grows sideways out of sight. To you that is distance, and it arrives with no argument, which makes it feel like he decided something without telling you. He didn't. He found the only move that didn't cost him his shape.

So check the sequence. If the quiet came right after you started steering, he is not gone. If it came after a plain ask, wait one solar term: the month pillar turns somewhere between the 4th and the 8th, and wood moves on month boundaries far more than on moods. This is the same clock that decides when a quiet man texts again.

strong wood, weak wood, and the one thing each needs

Born in spring — 인월 and 묘월, roughly 4 February to 4 April — the month branch usually gives him roots: strong wood, thick, growing, and convinced. Strong wood doesn't need more encouragement. It needs metal, which is to say pruning, which is to say someone who will say no and not move. He respects a boundary more than a compliment, and without one he grows over you and calls it shade.

Born in autumn — 신월 and 유월 — usually the opposite: thin wood, the branch that got cut back. He needs water, which is rest, quiet, and a person who does not add heat. Push a weak wood man and you get agreement with no content, a man who says yes to everything and means none of it, and a relationship that ends with the words I never actually said that.

a december wood is a tree in frozen ground

Korean saju reads climate as well as quantity: 조후, johu. A wood day master born in winter, between roughly 7 December and 3 February, is a tree in frozen soil. Everything about him is slower and more formal than the element suggests, and he warms up on a schedule you cannot hurry. Put a warm, expressive person next to him and you get the only version of him that blooms early.

A summer wood, born 사월 or 오월, is the opposite: dry ground, too much sun, and no patience left for heat. He needs water — steadiness, quiet evenings, someone who doesn't perform. Same element, opposite prescription. No list of traits can do this part, because it isn't a trait. It's a month.

what his chart calls a wife

For a man, the spouse star is 재성, jaeseong, the wealth star: the element his day master controls. Wood controls earth. So a wood man's wife star is earth — 무토 and 기토, the mountain and the field.

Which means the woman who actually ends up holding him is usually the steady one. A routine, an opinion, a life that continues when he is late. Not the most electric person he has ever met. He is drawn to fire, but a wood man builds with earth, and those are two different women.

A wood man with no earth anywhere in the chart has nothing in the structure that says this one stays. He loves easily and stays lightly, and not out of cruelty: relationship is simply not native ground for him. A wood man with earth in his month or day pillar will mention a house before he mentions a holiday.

The chart names a role, never a verdict. Plenty of wood men without a gram of earth marry once and stay thirty years. What the earth tells you is where the staying would come from.

who he ends up with, by element

Water feeds him, so water feels like home. It is also what a chart reaches for when it is missing one, which is the other half of this reading and the reason the same woman keeps turning up. He will say I don't know why, but I'm calm with you and mean it. The risk is a man who leans forever and never grows a trunk.

Fire is what he grows toward. Admiration, heat, a woman who makes him want to be seen. He gives, she shines. It burns bright and it burns through him, so it lasts if she also lets him rest.

Earth is his wife star. She holds. He will drain her slowly unless somebody keeps score, and a wood man who has been drained from is a very quiet man with a very tidy garden.

Metal cuts him: friction, attraction, the woman he cannot manage and cannot quite leave. A strong wood man needs a little of it, the way a tree needs a hard winter to make a ring. These four are the raw pairs; gunghap scores them alongside the day branches and the year pillars, which is why one number comes out the other end rather than one word.

how a wood man leaves

He doesn't slam anything. He thins out: a word shorter every week, then a birthday message, then nothing you can point at. A tree that leaves does it by growing somewhere else, so there is rarely a scene to describe to your friends, which is its own kind of exhausting.

Months later the season turns and a hey arrives. If you have already had that text, it has a reading of its own. If you are in the thinning right now, what to do with the silence is a separate question, and the answer depends on whether it is his default or his decision.

what a chart will not tell you

Whether he is kind. Whether he means it this time. Whether the man you are reading is the man he is on a bad week, which is the only week that counts.

Saju gives you his default: the shape he returns to when nothing is pushing him. It does not choose for you, and a reading that offers to is selling you a worse product than this one. The three-times rule holds here too: one quiet month is weather. The same quiet every season for a year is a structure.

quick answers

What is a wood day master man like in love? Two kinds. 갑목 loves by building you into his plans and won't say the sentimental line unless asked. 을목 loves by noticing and adapting, and he is warm to everyone, so interest shows in repetition rather than enthusiasm.

How do I find out if he's wood? His birth date gives the day pillar, and the stem of that pillar is his day master. The hour changes the hour pillar, not the day stem, so a missing birth time doesn't block you.

Do wood men fall in love fast? Slowly, then for a long time. Yang wood decides early and admits it late. Yin wood looks quick because he is warm in week one, but he is still testing whether the connection is safe long after he looks settled.

Why does he go quiet? Usually a stalled growth rather than changed feelings. Managed, corrected in public, or pushed to decide on your schedule, wood stops instead of arguing. Check what was above his head that month.

What element is a wood man's wife? Earth. For a man the spouse star is 재성, and wood controls earth. Water feels like home to him, fire is what he grows toward, and earth is who he marries in the chart's language.
