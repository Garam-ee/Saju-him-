the words English is missing

Six Korean words for six things he is already doing. English named one of them. Each one comes with the element behind it — because the word tells you what he is doing, and the element tells you what happens next.

간보기 · gan-bo-gi

Breadcrumbing is “I don’t want to date you. I just don’t want you to leave.” 간보기 is “How much can I get away with before she says something?” Different job — one is maintenance, the other is measurement. 土 earth: the one who never changes, so testing costs him nothing and never stops on its own. Do: one ask with a real date on it. If the answer is “sometime,” you don’t ask again.

썸 · sseom

A situationship can run forever — that is the design. 썸 is a stage, and stages resolve. Yours feels illegal because nobody around you has a word for it. 木 wood: growing, not there yet — honest, and slow enough to cost you a year. Do: give it eight weeks in writing, to yourself, not to him.

밀당 · mil-dang

Hot and cold is “I don’t know what I want.” 밀당 is “I know exactly what I’m doing. I’m waiting for you to reach first.” One is a symptom, one is a technique. 水 water: silence for days, then a paragraph at 2am. Do: stop matching his tempo. Same warmth, your speed — including the good weeks.

어장관리 · eo-jang-gwal-li

“I haven’t picked yet” versus “I’ve picked. It’s not you. But stay where I can find you.” The word literally contains management, because it is work. 火 fire: everything for two weeks, then nothing — not cruelty, fire just cannot burn in one place. Do: ask for one thing that cannot be given to two people at once.

잠수 · jam-su

Ghosting is “I’m done. I’m not coming back.” 잠수 is “I’m not done. I’m just gone for a while.” English only gave you the permanent one, so every disappearance gets filed as permanent — and then you are blindsided in November. 水 water moves with temperature, not feelings. Do: write your terms for his return today, while you are still angry.

정 · jeong

“I want a future with you” versus “I don’t want a future with you. I just can’t undo four months.” English has words close to 정 — attachment, bond — but none of them describe a bond that keeps holding after the wanting stops. You were not stupid. You were 정. 土 earth accumulates; nobody falls in 정, you find out you are standing in it. Do: describe it as a stranger’s story, same facts, no names — then answer her.

So which one is he?

A word is a pattern, and a pattern is not a person. You just met five elements: five different men doing similar-looking things for completely different reasons, each leaving on a different schedule. Which one he is, which month he moves, and what your chart does to his — that part is calculated, not defined.
