Why it is called 9S

Nine seconds is the whole gap between his birthday going in and the answer coming out. Everything slow about saju — the appointment, the waiting room, the person asking why you want to know — is the part that got removed.

What comes out

A six-page PDF. Both charts in full with hidden stems, the five-element balance side by side, the gunghap score with every axis and every point shown, and a page on which of his behaviours are signal and which are just his default.

What it does not do

It will not tell you whether he comes back, and it does not score marriage. A chart describes two structures and where they rub. Anything past that is invention, and you can get invention for free.
